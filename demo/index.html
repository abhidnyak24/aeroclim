Model evidence, assumptions & demo notes

Calculated test errors — synthetic data only

Horizon Ridge MAE Persistence MAE Regional MAE Test rows

Three ridge regressions are trained in this browser on generated temperature-offset history. Inputs include present offset, trend, hour, surface properties, humidity and future regional temperature. There are no prewritten accuracy or savings results.

Training and testing are separated by time and forecast horizon. Both ridge and regional baselines receive the generated future regional temperature. Real weather forecast error, hardware error and transfer to real sites are not validated here. These numbers must not be presented as field accuracy.

The anomaly detector uses Holt level/trend residuals and requires two consecutive high residuals. The planner uses a beam search through off, half-power and full-power actions. Only the forecasting component learns regression coefficients.

Transparent assumptions

  • Four synthetic nodes; no USB, MQTT, ESP or sensor connection.
  • Room thermal time constant: 4 hours. Extra heat gain: 0.15°C/h. Full cooling effect: 2.6°C/h. Full electrical draw: 2.2 kW.
  • Illustrative tariffs: ₹8/kWh, rising to ₹14/kWh from scenario hour +2 to +4. These are not your actual local tariffs.
  • Reactive thermostat switches on at the comfort ceiling and off 1°C below it. Optimizer trades off cost, overheating, overcooling and switching.
  • Humidity and light are illustrative; no gas, particulate, CO₂ or carbon-emissions claims are generated.
  • Location selects regional weather, not a measurement of your room. Manual values are assumptions.

Weather and geocoding: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Online loading is optional; API availability is not guaranteed. Export run saves inputs, assumptions, generated results, metrics and model coefficients.
